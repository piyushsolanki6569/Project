# AquaPredict AI — Problem Statement

## Title
Smart Water Prediction & Management Using Data Analytics and AI

## Problem
Water consumption varies according to occupancy, season, weather, and daily activity. Campuses, buildings, and communities may not have a simple way to estimate upcoming demand or identify unusual usage early. As a result, resource teams may notice potential waste only after a bill rises, a shortage occurs, or an issue is reported.

## Proposed Idea
AquaPredict AI is a proposed dashboard that uses historical water-use data to estimate future demand and flag unusual readings for review. It starts with CSV data and can later be extended to connect to sensors or meters.

## Target Users
- Campus facility and maintenance teams
- Building managers
- Community water-management teams
- Sustainability coordinators

## MVP Scope
1. Upload historical water-use data.
2. Validate and clean the dataset.
3. Visualize usage trends.
4. Produce a baseline daily or weekly forecast.
5. Flag unusually high or low readings.
6. Display alerts and practical follow-up suggestions.

## Success Measures
The implemented prototype should be evaluated using:
- Forecast error on held-out data (for example, MAE).
- Quality of anomaly flags reviewed against known or manually verified events.
- Successful handling of missing, malformed, and insufficient data.
- Feedback from potential users about clarity and usefulness.

No performance or water-saving results are claimed at the idea stage.
